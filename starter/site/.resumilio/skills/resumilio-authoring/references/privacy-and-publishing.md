# Privacy and publishing

The public profile may contain facts the person approves, public contact routes, and external public links. It must not contain private resume files, certificate files, customer data, credentials, access tokens, local paths, unpublished source notes, or hidden implementation details.

For NDA-protected work, publish only a safe summary approved by the person. Do not name a customer or infer permission from the existence of private material.

Local preview and validation are reversible authoring steps. Deploying, publishing a package, changing a domain, reading a connected private service, or posting externally requires explicit authorization for that action.
