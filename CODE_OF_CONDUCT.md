# Code of conduct

We want Resumilio to be a welcoming project for people of different backgrounds and experience levels.

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish another person's private information, or misrepresent their work. Assume good intent while addressing harmful impact directly.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior undermines a safe, truthful, collaborative environment. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed with discretion and a proportionate response.
